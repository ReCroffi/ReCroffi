Trainer Pro Gym Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Overview

Trainer Pro Gym is a workout tracking app. It helps users plan workouts, log exercises, record sets, reps, weights, cardio notes, review history, and export or restore local backups.

Data Storage

Workout history, preferences, and backup data are stored locally on the user's device. Trainer Pro Gym does not require an account and does not upload workout history to developer servers by default.

Data Collection

Trainer Pro Gym does not sell personal data, does not use third-party advertising, and does not use user data for tracking across apps or websites.

If a user voluntarily sends support or feedback by email, the provided message and contact information are used only to respond to the request.

Health Disclaimer

Workout entries are user-provided fitness notes and are not medical advice. Users should consult a qualified professional before starting a new training program or making health decisions.

User Control

Users can export and restore local JSON backups and can clear local workout history. If the app is deleted or local device data is cleared without a backup, workout history may be lost.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trainerprohelp@gmail.com.
